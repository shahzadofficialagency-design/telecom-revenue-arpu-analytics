**Telecom Revenue & ARPU Analytics Dashboard — PakConnect Telecom**

I built a complete, end-to-end analytics project covering the full data lifecycle — from data generation to business insights:

🔹 Designed a realistic dataset: 10,000 customers and 109,369 monthly revenue records across 7 cities in Pakistan (Jan–Dec 2025, PKR)

🔹 Developed KPI logic for Revenue, ARPU, Churn Rate and Subscriber Growth — SQL results reconciled exactly with Python
🔹 Designed a 4-page Power BI dashboard (Executive Overview, Revenue, Subscriber & ARPU, Package Performance) with DAX measures

📊 Key findings from the analysis:
• Total Revenue: PKR 158.0M | Average Monthly ARPU: PKR 1,458
• Lahore led in total revenue, while Islamabad had the highest ARPU
• Data services contributed ~47% of total revenue
• Family Connect was the top package by revenue; Business Pro had the highest ARPU per customer
